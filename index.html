Common Problems We Fix

Does any of this sound familiar?

⚠️

Your guest Wi-Fi is on the same network as your registers

Most small business routers put everything on one network. That means a customer on your guest Wi-Fi can potentially see your point-of-sale system.

🔓

You have no firewall — just a router from the internet provider

ISP-provided routers have almost no security configuration. They are not firewalls. Your business data is not protected.

🔐

Nobody knows the camera password

The installer set it up, left, and now nobody has the credentials. The cameras work until they don't — and then nobody can get in to fix them.

📶

Wi-Fi dead zones are killing productivity

One router in the back office does not cover a 4,000 square foot building. Your employees know every dead spot by heart.

👻

You have no idea what is on your network

Old devices, vendor tablets, employee phones, and forgotten equipment all share your business connection with zero visibility or control.

📋

Your last IT person left and took the knowledge with them

No documentation, no passwords written down, no network map. Starting from scratch every time something breaks.

🐢

Everything works — but everything feels slow

Slow networks are not always a hardware problem. Misconfigured routing, bloated firewall rules, and unmanaged traffic can cut your network performance in half. We find it and fix it.

If you recognized even one of these, it is worth a conversation.
