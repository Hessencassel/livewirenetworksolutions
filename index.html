Pillar 2

Network Security

Wi-Fi, firewalls, switching, and cabling that hold up under real use — and are documented well enough that the next tech can support them.

  • Firewalls & threat protection Multi-vendor expertise across Palo Alto, Fortinet, SonicWall, and Cisco — proper rules, NAT, and threat prevention.
  • Wi-Fi design & install Whole-home and whole-building wireless that reaches every corner. Ubiquiti UniFi and Cisco Meraki specialists.
  • Network segmentation VLANs and access controls that separate guest, IoT, staff, and POS traffic — so one device can't take down the rest.
  • Structured cabling Clean Cat6 / Cat6A and fiber runs, labeled and tested, that an electrician would compliment.
  • VPN & remote access Site-to-site and client VPNs that actually stay up — IPSec, SSL, and ZTNA done right.

About Livewire

Enterprise-grade thinking. Small-business pricing.

Livewire was founded by technicians who spent careers building and securing networks for large organizations — and got tired of seeing small businesses and homeowners get either overcharged or underserved.

We bring more than 60 years of combined experience from enterprise IT and security environments — the kind of work where downtime costs real money and a camera system either holds up or it doesn't. That's the bar we hold ourselves to on every job, whether it's a single home or a busy storefront.
