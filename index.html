About Livewire

A small crew with a deep enterprise background.

Livewire was started by technicians who spent careers building and securing networks for large organizations — and got tired of seeing small businesses and homeowners get either overcharged or underserved.

We bring more than 60 years of combined experience from enterprise IT and security environments — the kind of work where downtime costs real money and a camera system either holds up or it doesn't. That's the bar we hold ourselves to on every job, whether it's a single home or a busy storefront.

What you get is straightforward: an honest assessment, a written quote with no mystery line items, a clean install you can be proud of, and someone who answers the phone when you call. We don't subcontract the actual work and we don't disappear after the invoice is paid.

We treat your network and your cameras like we'd want ours treated — done once, done right, and easy to live with.
